BTS

BTS is a South Korean boy band group also known as Bangtan Sonyeondan which means ""Bulletproof Boy Scouts," representing protection of youth against societal pressures.

The group was formed in 2013 and has since become one of the most popular K-pop groups in the world.

BTS consists of seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook. They are known for their powerful performances, meaningful lyrics, and strong connection with their fans, known as ARMY.

BTS has released several successful albums and singles, including "Dynamite," "Butter," and "Permission to Dance." They have also won numerous awards and have been recognized for their contributions to music and culture.

In addition to their music, BTS is also known for their philanthropic efforts, including their partnership with UNICEF for the "Love Myself" campaign, which aims to end violence against children and teenagers.

BTS continues to break records and make history in the music industry, and their influence extends beyond music, inspiring fans around the world with their messages of self-love, mental health awareness, and social issues.
